Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is FRP (Factory Reset Protection)?

FRP is a security feature on Android devices that prevents unauthorized access after a factory reset by requiring the previously synced Google account login.

2. Is bypassing FRP legal?

Bypassing FRP is legal if you're doing it on a device that you personally own, or have proper permission to unlock. It is illegal to bypass FRP on stolen or unauthorized devices.

3. What does this website offer?

We provide legal, step-by-step instructions and helpful tools to assist users in unlocking their own devices when they forget their Google account after a reset.

4. Do I need a PC to unlock my device?

It depends on the brand and Android version. Some methods require only an APK, while others (especially Samsung) may need a computer and USB cable.

5. What are APK tools?

APK tools are Android apps used to help access certain phone settings or bypass login screens. We only provide safe, tested tools for personal use.

6. Can I use this on someone else's phone?

No. That is not allowed. This website is strictly for educational and personal-use only. Using these methods on stolen or unauthorized devices is illegal.

7. Is your content safe?

Yes. All our guides and files are manually reviewed for safety. Always scan APKs before installing and only download from trusted sources.
